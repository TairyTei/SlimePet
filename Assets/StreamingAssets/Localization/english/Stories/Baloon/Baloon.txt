Today seemed extremely lazy for the little slime.  
$pet_name had done almost nothing since morning but lie in bed, snoring sweetly. Their snores echoed through the house, but this couldn't last forever. Opening sleepy eyes, the slime stretched their mouth wide in a yawn.  

— Nyaa-aah, — escaped the little one's lips.  

Sleep hadn't fully released its grip, yet $pet_name had already resolved to embark on a new adventure. The town where they lived with their owner wasn't large, but overflowed with interesting places and equally fascinating people. Just yesterday: the owner took the pet for a walk in the park, where $pet_name saw a marvelous sight—colorful balloons floating above people's heads! And deep in the park was someone selling these wonderful things. The little one desperately wanted one, so without a second thought, they slipped out through the pet door recently installed by the owner.  

The park felt just as enchanting as before. Lush greenery filled the air with nature's freshness. Vibrant flowers and equally bright flowerbeds delighted the eye, attracting all sorts of pollinating insects. $pet_name, previously following their route, chased a red butterfly. So engrossed, they wandered into an unfamiliar part of the park. No one was around—only the wind howling eerily, frightening the slime. Trees that once felt reassuring now loomed like lurking giants. Dry branches resembled gnarled hands reaching for the poor little one.  

— Hello? — $pet_name called out, glancing around.  

The slime felt piercing eyes watching from behind the trees. No answer came. $pet_name curled into a tiny mucus ball and hid under a bench, trying to escape whatever was observing them. Minutes dragged like hours.  

— Everything alright? — came a weary voice from above.  

$pet_name opened their eyes. Before them stood the balloon seller himself, holding an ordinary red balloon. The man looked concerned.  

— I... I'm lost, — the slime whispered, crawling out.  

— I'm lost too, — the seller smiled, glancing at the balloon, — but you know? Eventually, you'll find your landmark.  

He nodded toward the trees. Between the trunks, a red balloon flickered—a path where people walked. The man stood, gently scooping $pet_name into his hands. The slime didn't resist. With another tired smile, he offered them the balloon.  

— For me? — $pet_name asked hesitantly.  

The man nodded. Accepting the gift, $pet_name walked beside him toward the path, soon reaching the park entrance.  

— Won't get lost now? — he asked, lowering the slime to the ground.  

$pet_name shook their head emphatically. Unable to contain joy, they bounced around the man. His loud laughter seemed to lift shadows of weariness and sadness from his face.  

— Thank you! See you around! — $pet_name called, heading toward the exit.  

— Good luck, — the seller waved, walking back into the park.
