The sun was blazing fiercely that day, as if someone had tossed a whole heap of coal into the celestial furnace. Despite the clear sky and beautiful weather, $pet_name and Basilio took shelter in the treehouse, seeking refuge from the heat under its green roof. The slime had melted across the floorboards, nearly turning into a puddle. The portly cat sprawled his bulk beside the panoramic window, lazily swishing his tail from side to side.
– On such a scor-r-rching day... Pastries ought to come straight to my paws, – purred the cat, leisurely placing his fluffy paw in a sunbeam that pierced the oak canopy.

A shadow flickered at the window. The sound of wings and cheerful chirping instantly revealed it was Chirik! Perching on the very edge, the sparrow hopped joyfully left and right, chirping his friend’s name.

– $pet_name, $pet_name! Hi, $pet_name! I’m back, didn’t expect me? Oh, – The bird spotted the cat purring lazily below, – And who’s this whiskered fellow lurking here? Is he with you?

The slime pulled itself from the puddle back into its original form. Bouncing on the spot, it sprang toward its friend. Basilio, meanwhile, yawned dramatically, not even glancing at Chirik.

– Yes, that’s Basilio! My friend. Basilio, this is Chirik. Also my friend.

The cat opened his eyes and peered at the bird. His lazy gaze suddenly sharpened with predatory zeal. Squinting slyly, he beckoned Chirik with a still paw.

– Birdy, oh birdy? Come here. I’ve got a yummy wor-r-rm for you.

– A worm? – Chirik asked naively, fluttering closer to the sly whiskerface.

Basilio let out a loud "Mee-OW!" and lunged at the sparrow. Chirik, however, didn’t panic and darted out the window. $pet_name, horrified, tried to stop his pals—but Basilio’s instincts had clearly taken over. The tubby tabby leapt with surprising agility onto the oak’s thick branch, then to the next. With the elegance innate to all cats, he landed on the fence and made a nimble jump, trying to snatch Chirik as he flitted over the yard. But the spirited bird deftly dodged, and the gray furball tumbled onto the grass.

– Well, whiskerface, catch me if you can! – laughed the sparrow, zipping around the yard.

Not the least bit deterred, the cat gave chase. He tore across the yard like a meteor through space: past the fountain, behind the flowerbed, past the yellow inflatable pool $owner_name had recently ordered. Slipping on wet splashes, the cat slammed full-tilt into the rubber wall. BOOM! Basilio collapsed wearily onto the grass, gasping for breath. Soon, the anxious slime descended via elevator. It squelched toward its friend and whispered:

– Everything okay?

Suddenly, Chirik landed on the weary feline’s head. Puffing out his little chest triumphantly, he settled comfortably atop the loser and chirped smugly:

– You give up, whiskerface?

– I give up, – Basilio wheezed.

His feline instincts seemed finally subdued. Whether it was the chase, his ample belly, or the solid whack against the pool—he clearly had no plans left to attack the sparrow.

– I’m sorry, Chirik, – $pet_name whispered sadly, trying to apologize for his friend.

– Are you kidding? I loved it! Tag is what friends play! Let’s meet every Wednesday for this! – chirped the bird merrily, fluttering to the fountain.

– I’m in, – murmured Basilio without rising from the cool grass, – High time I started shedding this.

He patted his thick flank. The friends burst out laughing. They spent the rest of the day playing together until evening fell and it was time to part.
