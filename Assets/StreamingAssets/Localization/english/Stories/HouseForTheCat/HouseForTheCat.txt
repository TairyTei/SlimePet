— I see the oak, I see the bucket... but how do we actually get to this treehouse? — Basilio eyed the makeshift elevator.

— Like this! — $pet_name grinned, hopping inside the bucket.  

The gray cat cautiously joined, watching the slime. $pet_name shut the door and pressed the button. A motor hummed above as the rope lifted them. Basilio flinched but quickly adjusted, peering over the edge as the yard shrank below. Branches came into view, revealing a simple yet sturdy wooden structure.

— Truly, — purred the cat, — So I can live here?  

The slime nodded. Settling onto the cushion, they basked in sunlight. Basilio joined. They lounged silently, soaking up warmth and companionship. From the owner’s house drifted the song "Blue Sky".  

— I’d gladly stay, $pet_name, — Basilio stood at last, — Thank you. And thank your owner.  

He wound around the slime, rubbing his whiskered cheek against them. $pet_name answered with a purring hum. The cat broke into a wide smile.
